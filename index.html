About InnovateX 2026

An immersive conclave uniting the computational power of the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (RAS) with the industrial rigor of the IEEE Industry Applications Society (IAS).

Robotics & Automation Society (RAS)

Cultivating innovation in cybernetic autonomy, drone systems, AI perception, and automated algorithmic problem solving for modern physical computing.

  • Autonomous System Design
  • Computer Vision & Kinematics

Industry Applications Society (IAS)

Bridging theoretical academia with industrial deployment: IoT architectures, renewable smart microgrids, motor drives, and mission-critical hardware.

  • Industrial IoT & Cloud Integration
  • Sustainable Power & Automation

MITS Gwalior Heritage

Hosted at Madhav Institute of Technology & Science, Gwalior — empowering student innovators through premier technical societies and research initiatives.

  • Hands-on Lab Collaborations
  • Recognized IEEE Chapter Impact

Grand Opening & Keynote 1: Autonomous Robotics in Industry 4.0

Focusing on the transition toward resilient, zero-emission industrial power architecture, grid modernization, edge automation, and intelligent power electronics in smart factories.
